Key Relationships

  • Employee → Shifts (1:M): One employee can work multiple shifts
  • Shift → Transactions (1:M): One shift contains multiple transactions
  • Transaction → Items (1:M): One transaction has multiple line items
  • Shift → Cash Movements (1:M, CASCADE): Cash movements deleted with shift

Variance Interpretation

  • Zero (0): Perfect reconciliation ✅
  • Positive (+300): Cash OVER - Extra money in drawer ⚠️
  • Negative (-300): Cash SHORT - Missing money ❌

Procedure Descriptions

  • sp_start_shift: Opens new shift with smart numbering (YYYYMMDD-LOC1-EMP2-001)
  • sp_create_pos_transaction: Atomically creates transaction and deducts inventory
  • sp_void_pos_transaction: Voids transaction and restores inventory (manager only)
  • sp_close_shift: Closes shift with automatic variance calculation
